Our Story

Built on one belief — purity is not negotiable.

Spirucraft began in Telangana with a simple goal: grow spirulina the honest way, and let the algae yield two distinct, high-value products instead of one.

Who We Are

A young company, an old craft

SPIRUCRAFT was founded with a shared vision — to harness the power of microalgae and deliver premium-quality spirulina products that meet global standards. Built on a foundation of innovation, sustainability, and quality, the company combines operational excellence with customer-centric business development to serve clients across domestic and international markets.

Established in 2024 and based in Medak, Telangana, we specialize in Spirulina Powder and Phycocyanin — combining controlled cultivation with reliable production practices for the nutraceutical, food, beverage, animal nutrition and aquaculture sectors.

How We Work

Pond to pouch, in seven stages

All stages are carried out under controlled conditions to maintain nutritional integrity, purity, and consistency.

Stage 01

Cultivation

Spirulina biomass is grown in our raceway tanks at Telangana under controlled, monitored conditions.

Stage 02

Harvesting

Biomass moves from the raceway tanks into dedicated harvest ponds, then through dewatering and cleaning.

Stage 03

Controlled Drying

Sun and solar drying across dedicated drying areas, keeping temperatures low to protect nutritional value.

Stage 04

Pulverizing

Dried biomass is milled in our pulverizer room into a fine powder — 100% passing 80 mesh.

Stage 05

Quality Testing

Every batch is checked for protein, moisture, microbial load and heavy metals in our storage & testing room.

Stage 06

Bulk Packaging

Packed in 10kg, 25kg or 50kg formats in our dedicated packaging room, ready for dispatch.

Stage 07

Delivery

Shipped to nutraceutical, pharma, food, feed and aquaculture buyers worldwide, with logistics support included.

Site Plan

How the facility is laid out

Our Telangana unit sits on roughly 1,640 sq. yards, laid out so biomass moves in one direction — cultivation, harvest, drying, processing — without backtracking.

Reading the layout

4 raceway tanks, 20ft × 150ft each
4 dedicated harvest ponds, one per tank
2 drying areas + a solar dryer
Dewatering & cleaning area
Pulverizer room for milling
Storage & quality testing room
Minerals storage & packaging room
Stock room for raw material receiving

Biomass grows in the raceway tanks, flows down into the harvest ponds, then moves up to the drying areas along the side of the plot. From there it goes to the pulverizer room to be milled into powder, across to quality testing, and finally into the packaging room for dispatch — a straight, one-way path from pond to pouch, over a plot measuring 96ft × 163ft.
